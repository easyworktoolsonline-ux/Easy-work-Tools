Privacy Policy

EasyWorkTools.online is a free online utility website that provides browser-based tools, tutorials and related resources. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you visit or use the website and how we handle it.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask visitors to create an account to use the public tools. Some tools are designed to process inputs directly in your browser. Where a tool performs processing locally, the content you enter is not intentionally sent to our server by that tool.

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address, tool name, browser details or the description of an issue. We use that information only to respond, provide support and improve the website.

2. Automatically collected technical information

Like most websites, our hosting, security, performance and advertising providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, approximate location, referring URL, pages viewed and timestamps. The exact information available depends on the services enabled on the site and your browser settings.

3. Cookies, local storage and similar technologies

EasyWorkTools.online may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies for essential site functionality, preferences, measurement or advertising when those services are enabled. Individual tools may also use browser storage to remember settings or process data locally.

4. Google AdSense and advertising

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled on the website, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on users' visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. Users can manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. Users can also learn about options for third-party personalized advertising at aboutads.info. These disclosures are included to address Google publisher requirements; applicable privacy and consent laws may require additional notices or choices.

5. European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland

Where required, we will use an appropriate consent mechanism for advertising and other non-essential cookies or local storage. Google states that publishers serving personalized ads to users in the EEA, UK or Switzerland must use a Google-certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. We will configure Google's Privacy & Messaging tools or another appropriate certified solution before serving personalized ads to users in regions where those requirements apply.

6. How we use information

Information may be used to operate and secure the website, respond to support requests, diagnose technical issues, improve tools and content, understand site performance, and deliver or measure advertising where advertising has been enabled.

7. Third-party services and links

The website may link to third-party services or websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites that we do not control.

8. Data retention

We retain support emails and related information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain support records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or protect the website and its users.

9. Children's privacy

The website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us by email, please contact us so we can address the request.

10. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability of personal information. You can contact us about a privacy request using the email address below. We may need enough information to verify and process a request.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the service. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website, services, advertising setup or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email EasyWorkTools.online@gmail.com.
