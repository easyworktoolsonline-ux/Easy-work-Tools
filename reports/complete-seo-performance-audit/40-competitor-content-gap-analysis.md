## What competitors offer that we should evaluate

- Wider conversion format coverage and deep format-specific pages.
- More explicit "convert X to Y" intent matching and examples.
- Stronger topical clusters around PDF, image and audio/video conversion.
- More resource-page and editorial link acquisition.

## What EasyWorkTools can realistically do better

- Browser-only/privacy-first positioning where technically true.
- Clearer worked examples and FAQ coverage on existing tools.
- Better internal linking from guides to tools and between complementary tools.
- Original explanations that do not copy competitor content.
