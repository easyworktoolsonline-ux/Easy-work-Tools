# 43 — White-hat backlink outreach strategy

Research and documentation only. **No outreach emails, messages, submissions, purchases or link placements were performed.**

## Ethical principles

- No PBNs, spam directories, automated outreach, fake profiles, paid placements without explicit approval, blog-comment spam or link farms.
- No contacting anyone as part of this research.
- Prioritize genuine editorial fit, useful resources and original data.

## Recommended linkable assets (hypotheses, no implementation)

1. A privacy-first browser-only tool explainer that clearly states what runs locally and what does not.
2. Original, cited research on browser-only tool performance or format-conversion edge cases.
3. A free resource page for students/developers with working examples.
4. Format-specific guides that link to the matching tool rather than duplicate it.

## Prospect types

- Developer resources and documentation ecosystems.
- Educational how-to and reference sites.
- Technology/productivity publications.
- Genuine tool directories with editorial review.
- Partnership or resource pages where the tool solves a documented visitor need.

## Outreach templates (not sent)

### Resource-page suggestion
```text
Subject: Resource suggestion for [page/topic]

Hello [name],

I found your resource page on [topic]. If it is useful for your readers, one possibly relevant addition is our free browser-based [tool] at [URL]. It lets users [specific task] without [accurate limitation].

No action is needed if the page is closed to suggestions.

Best,
[name]
```

### Original-research pitch
```text
Subject: Original data for your [topic] article

Hello [name],

We published an original, cited dataset on [topic]. If it is relevant to your audience, here is the link: [URL]. Feel free to use the data with attribution.

Best,
[name]
```

No emails were sent and no contact list was actioned.
