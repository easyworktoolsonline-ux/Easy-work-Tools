## Link quality and suspicious patterns

- Followed referring domains: 1 (0.5%); nofollow 183 (99.5%).
- Followed backlinks: 4 (2.1%); nofollow 183 (97.9%).
- Observed link UR values: all below 10.
- Top referring domains were marked SPAM, including `scamadviser.com` and many `.shop`, `.site` and `.store` domains with SEO/PBN-like names.
- Investigation needed before any action: the suspicious pattern is consistent with low-quality link acquisition or spam references, but tool labels alone are not grounds for disavow. No disavow or link removal was performed.
