## 2. Link quality

SEMrush:

- All 42 referring domains fall in Authority Score band 0–20.
- 8 new nofollow, low-Authority-Score referring domains were observed in Oct 2026.
- Lost filter: `Nothing found`.
- Several referring domains use low-trust TLDs such as `.xyz`, `.shop`, `.site`.

Ahrefs:

- Followed referring domains: **1 of 184 (0.5%)**.
- Nofollow referring domains: **183 (99.5%)**.
- Followed backlinks: **4 of 187 (2.1%)**.
- Nofollow backlinks: **183 (97.9%)**.
- All observed link UR values are below 10.
- Top referring domains were marked **SPAM**; the highest-authority example was `scamadviser.com` (DR 80) with 4 links, while most others were low-quality `.shop`/`.site`/`.store` domains with DR roughly 48–58 and PBN-like patterns.

Conclusion: the profile is sparse, almost entirely nofollow, and low trust. There is no evidence of a strong editorial or directory link base in the sampled data.

Any future off-site work would require separate owner approval. This audit makes **no** outreach, purchase, paid placement, disavow, or irreversible backlink recommendation as an action item.
