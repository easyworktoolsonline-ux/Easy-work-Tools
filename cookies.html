Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how cookies, local storage and similar technologies may be used on EasyWorkTools.online.

1. Essential functionality

Some browser storage may be used to remember preferences or support features of individual tools. Many tools are designed to work without sending user-entered content to our servers.

2. Analytics and measurement

If analytics services are enabled, they may use cookies or similar technologies to understand how visitors use the website, measure performance and identify technical issues. Any enabled analytics service will be disclosed in the Privacy Policy and handled according to applicable consent requirements.

3. Advertising cookies

If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver and measure advertising. Google states that its advertising cookies can be used to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Users can manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings and learn about some third-party opt-out choices at aboutads.info.

4. Consent

Where consent is legally required, we will provide an appropriate consent mechanism before using non-essential cookies or local storage for the relevant purposes. Google provides Privacy & Messaging tools for publishers, and Google requires a certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework for personalized ads in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. We will configure the appropriate solution before serving personalized ads in those regions.

5. Managing cookies

You can usually control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies or storage may affect website functionality.

6. Contact

Questions about cookies or privacy can be sent to EasyWorkTools.online@gmail.com.
