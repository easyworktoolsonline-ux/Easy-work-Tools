Pinterest Pin & Video Thumbnail Downloader: Save Any Pin Image Free

Every Pinterest Pin — whether a static image Pin or a video Pin — displays a cover image on boards and in search results, which is often what people mean by a Pinterest "thumbnail."

What is a Pinterest Pin thumbnail?

For an image Pin, the thumbnail is simply the Pin's own image at whatever resolution Pinterest has stored for it. For a video Pin, it's the static cover frame Pinterest displays before the video plays, similar to a YouTube or TikTok thumbnail.

How to download a Pinterest Pin thumbnail

  1. Open the public Pin and copy its link from the browser address bar or the Pin's Share button.
  2. Paste the link into Easy Thumbnail Downloader.
  3. The tool retrieves the Pin's publicly available cover image.
  4. Preview the result and click Download.

Image Pins vs. video Pins

Because a static image Pin's "thumbnail" and its full content are often the same file, downloading it gives you the Pin's original image. A video Pin's cover, on the other hand, is a separate still image generated by Pinterest and is what Easy Thumbnail Downloader retrieves for that Pin type.

Common uses for downloaded Pinterest thumbnails

  • Building a mood board or design reference collection.
  • Researching what cover styles perform well in a specific niche.
  • Archiving inspiration before a board or Pin is removed.
