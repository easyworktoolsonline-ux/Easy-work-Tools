How to Convert Text Case Online: Uppercase, Lowercase, Title Case & More

Need to clean up text quickly? An online text case converter can turn inconsistent copy into a consistent format in seconds.

What is text case conversion?

Text case conversion changes the capitalization pattern of existing text without requiring you to retype it. It is useful for documents, product data, headings, filenames, notes, social content and other everyday writing tasks.

How to use the EasyWorkTools.online text converter

  1. Open the Online Text Case Converter.
  2. Type or paste your text into the editor, or import a plain text file.
  3. Choose the case you need, such as UPPERCASE, lowercase or Title Case.
  4. Review the converted text and use Copy Text or Download Text when finished.

Common conversion options

  • UPPERCASE: makes every letter capital.
  • lowercase: makes every letter lowercase.
  • Title Case: capitalizes the beginning of words.
  • Capitalized Words: capitalizes word starts while preserving the rest of the text.
  • Alternating Case: alternates lowercase and uppercase characters.
  • slug-case: creates a compact, URL-friendly style.
  • snake_case: joins words with underscores.

When is an online text converter useful?

It can save time when you receive text from different sources and need a consistent format. It is especially handy for web content, spreadsheets, product catalogs, labels, drafts and developer-friendly text formats.

Why use a browser-based converter?

A browser tool is convenient because you can use it immediately without installing desktop software. EasyWorkTools.online is designed for quick, simple workflows so you can paste text, transform it and continue with your work.
