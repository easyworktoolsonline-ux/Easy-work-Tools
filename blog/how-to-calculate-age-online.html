How to Calculate Your Exact Age Online (Years, Months & Days)

"How old am I, exactly?" sounds like a simple question, but the honest answer is rarely a round number. Between one birthday and the next, your precise age is a mix of years, months and days that changes every single day — and getting it right matters for anything from a visa application to a school enrolment form.

Why "just subtract the years" doesn't work

The instinct is to subtract your birth year from the current year. That works on your birthday, and it's wrong every other day of the year. If you were born in July and it's currently March, a simple year subtraction overstates your age by one, because your next birthday hasn't happened yet. Getting an exact age right means comparing full dates, not just years, and correctly handling the fact that months have different lengths and some years have an extra day in February.

How to calculate your exact age with Age Calculator

  1. Open Age Calculator and enter your date of birth.
  2. Leave the comparison date set to today, or change it to any other date you want to measure against.
  3. The tool instantly shows your age as a precise years, months and days figure, plus totals in weeks and days.
  4. Scroll down to see how many days remain until your next birthday.

Real situations where an exact age matters

  • Legal and government forms. Visa, passport and benefits applications often ask for age "as of" a specific date, not today — a plain subtraction gets this wrong roughly a third of the time.
  • School and sports eligibility. Age-cutoff dates for a school year or a youth sports league depend on the exact day a child turns a certain age, not the calendar year they were born in.
  • Milestone tracking. Parents often want to know a baby's age in weeks or months rather than years during the first two years, since development is usually discussed in those units.
  • Countdowns and anniversaries. Knowing exactly how many days until a birthday, anniversary or retirement date is a common, purely practical use of the same calculation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days have I been alive?

Enter your date of birth into Age Calculator with today as the comparison date, and it totals every day since you were born alongside the same figure in weeks and months. Most people are surprised how large the day count is compared with the year count — for example, turning 30 means passing roughly 10,950 days.

How do I calculate the age gap between two people?

Set the date of birth to the older person, then change the comparison date to the younger person's date of birth instead of today. The result is the exact gap in years, months and days, rather than a rounded "about X years" estimate.

Why do two age calculators sometimes give different answers?

Differences usually come from how months are counted. Some tools average a month as 30.4 days and round, which drifts by a day or two around month boundaries. A calculator that counts real calendar months and adjusts for each month's actual length — including leap years — matches what you'd count by hand on a calendar.

Can I calculate age as of a future date?

Yes. Set the comparison date to any future date to see exactly how old someone will be on that day, which is useful for eligibility cut-offs, enrolment deadlines or planning a milestone birthday in advance.
