Frequently Asked Questions

Is the output a real PCM WAV file?

Yes. The tool writes a valid 44-byte RIFF/WAVE header and 16-bit signed little-endian PCM samples.

Can I convert multiple audio files at once?

Yes. Select multiple files and they will be bundled into a single ZIP download.
