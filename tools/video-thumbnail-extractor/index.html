Extract Crisp Video Thumbnails & Frame Sequences Locally

Capture high-resolution still frames from MP4, WebM, or MOV videos directly inside your browser without uploading large video files. Scrub to the exact millisecond you want or automatically extract evenly spaced frames and download them together in a ZIP archive.

Creators, editors, and social media managers use this tool to grab YouTube cover frames, inspect animation keyframes, and generate storyboard previews at native video resolution.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Drop or select any MP4, WebM, or MOV video file from your device.
  2. Use the video scrubber to preview a specific timestamp, or choose an interval (e.g., every 2 seconds) for batch extraction.
  3. Select your preferred image format (PNG for lossless stills or JPG for smaller file size).
  4. Download individual frames or click Extract All to save a ZIP archive of thumbnails.

Frequently Asked Questions

What video formats are supported for thumbnail extraction?

Any video format your browser can play natively, including MP4 (H.264), WebM (VP8/VP9), and MOV.

Are extracted thumbnails full resolution?

Yes. Frames are drawn onto an HTML5 Canvas matching the video’s intrinsic width and height.

Can I extract multiple thumbnails automatically?

Yes. Use the interval batch extractor to capture frames every N seconds and download them as a single ZIP.

Is my video uploaded to a server?

No. All frame decoding and image encoding happen locally in your browser.
