Compress & Resize Videos Privately in Your Browser

Reduce oversized MP4, WebM, or MOV video files for email attachments, Slack, Discord, or web embedding without sending private footage to third-party servers. Control maximum output width (1080p, 720p, 480p), target video bitrate, and optionally strip the audio track for even smaller files.

Once encoding completes, inspect side-by-side original vs. compressed file sizes, preview the result directly in the player, and download your compressed WebM video immediately.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Upload an MP4, WebM, or MOV video by dragging it into the dropzone or clicking to browse.
  2. Select a target maximum width (e.g., 1280px or 720px) and video bitrate.
  3. Choose whether to keep or mute the audio track.
  4. Click Compress Video, monitor real-time progress, preview the output, and download.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does browser-based video compression work?

Your browser decodes the source video, scales each frame onto an HTML5 Canvas at your chosen resolution, and re-encodes it with MediaRecorder at the selected bitrate.

Why does compression run in real time?

Browser MediaRecorder encodes video frames at 1x playback speed to keep video and audio tracks synchronized.

What output format does Video Compressor produce?

It outputs a widely supported WebM (VP9/VP8) video file playable in all modern browsers and media players.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Compression runs 100% locally on your device.
