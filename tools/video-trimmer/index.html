Trim & Cut Video Clips Without Uploading

Cut unwanted intros, outros, or dead air from MP4, WebM, and MOV videos right in your browser. Set exact start and end timestamps in seconds, optionally mute the audio track, and export a clean trimmed clip.

Because processing happens locally via HTML5 Canvas and MediaRecorder, even sensitive screen recordings and personal clips stay completely private on your device.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Select or drop a video file into the trimmer workspace.
  2. Enter or scrub to your desired Start Time and End Time in seconds.
  3. Optionally choose to mute audio in the trimmed output.
  4. Click Trim Video, preview the resulting clip, and download it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I trim a long video into a short 10-second clip?

Yes. Set the start and end timestamps to your 10-second window and only that segment is recorded and exported.

Can I remove audio while trimming?

Yes, select the mute audio option before clicking Trim Video.

Does this work on mobile browsers?

It works on modern desktop and Android Chrome/Edge/Firefox browsers that support MediaRecorder and captureStream.

Is there any watermark added to trimmed videos?

No. Your exported clip is completely watermark-free.
